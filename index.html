The story

Born from a lifelong love of underground electronic music

Neurotrition is Devon's premier destination for authentic psychedelic trance, founded by close friends and resident DJs Acid Drop Anton and PsyCartel. What started as a shared vision has grown into a fixture of the South West club scene.

Based at our spiritual home, The Attic in Torquay, we turn ordinary nights into immersive, high-energy sonic journeys — cutting-edge sound, mind-bending psychedelic decor, and a welcoming community of stompers.

From driving psychedelic beats to deep melodic frequencies, we keep the dancefloor moving until the early hours. Whether you're a seasoned psy-veteran or stepping into the UV glow for the first time — welcome to the family.

Meet the crew

Resident DJs

The people behind the decks — decades of underground experience between them.

Acid Drop Anton
Founder & resident · Maharetta Records

DJing since 1996, Anton started out as DJ Drop with a residency for Resonance in Southampton, playing alongside D&B legends like Brian Gee, Kenny Ken, Randal and Micky Finn. He found psytrance in 2001 and fully converted after Antaris Project 2003 in Germany. He plays anything from progressive up to full-on 148bpm and has shared bills with Eatstatic, Scorb, Mr Peculiar, Psymmetrix, Chris Rich, M Theory, Regan and more, at festivals including Boomtown, Nozstock, Goa Cream and Tribal Sphere.

SoundCloud Facebook
PsyCartel
Co-founder & resident · Phantasm Records — Luke Gendall

Signed to UK Psychedelic & Goa Trance label Phantasm Records, Luke co-promotes Neurotrition, which celebrated its 10th anniversary in 2025. He's smashed dancefloors nationwide alongside Eat Static, Scorb, Lucas, Dirty Saffi, Chris Rich, M-Theory and more. Known for versatile, high-energy sets — trancey progressive through to thumping, groovy, bassline-laden journeys — he's regularly found at Celtic Tribe, Spicey Mandala, Spellbound and other UK events and festivals.

Facebook (DJ PsyCartel) Facebook (Phantasm Records) SoundCloud
Xenoben
Producer & DJ — Benjamin Gildersleve

UK-based psychedelic trance producer and DJ crafting hypnotic, polychrome soundscapes — twisted ethnic textures, euphoric melodies and persistent, unpredictable, psychedelic vocal manipulation. Come and join the fun.
